Understanding the Ottawa Infrastructure Stress Simulation

1. The Domain: Why Summer Infrastructure Stress?

While winter pipe freezes are well known, summer heatwaves and dry spells create critical soil and hydraulic stress in the Ottawa region:

  • Leda Clay Soil Shrinkage: Ottawa sits on sensitive marine clay that desiccates and shrinks during prolonged dry spells, shifting underground soils and exerting shear forces on pipe networks.
  • Vintage Building & Infrastructure Proxy: Older neighborhoods developed before 1980 typically correlate with legacy cast-iron and ductile-iron utility installations that are more brittle under ground settlement.
  • Atmospheric & Demand Strain: Sustained high heat indexes accelerate evaporation and drive high municipal water distribution demand.

Project Purpose: Provide an extensible educational simulation and machine learning pipeline demonstrating how geospatial aggregation (Uber H3) and environmental features can model infrastructure vulnerability.

2. How to Read the Map

The map aggregates Ottawa into Uber H3 hexagons (Resolution 8, ~0.73 km² per cell — neighborhood block resolution).

Low (< 30%)

Baseline stability. Low linear asset density, modern building vintage, or mild environmental conditions.

Medium (30–69%)

Moderate stress. Moderate building age, increased linear pipe exposure, or emerging dry weather.

High (≥ 70%)

Elevated vulnerability hotspot. High concentration of pre-1980 legacy structures combined with severe dry/heat conditions.

3. Key Feature Inputs Explained

🚰 Water Mains (km)

Total linear kilometers of water distribution mains within the H3 cell.

🏛️ Pre-1980 Building Vintage (%)

Proportion of building footprints constructed before 1980, serving as an urban age proxy.

🌡️ Heat Index (°C)

Atmospheric heat metric combining temperature and relative humidity.

☀️ Consecutive Dry Days

Cumulative days without precipitation (< 1 mm), correlating with soil desiccation.

4. Scenario Simulator ("What-if")

  1. Locate the What-if scenario panel on the left sidebar.
  2. Adjust the Temperature and Consecutive dry days sliders.
  3. Click Apply scenario to recompute risk scores across the visible grid.
  4. Click any hexagon on the map to inspect its infrastructure features.

5. Simulation Notice & Data Provenance

This system operates in synthetic simulation mode. Training targets and mock features are generated for local offline development. It is an educational engineering prototype, not an operational municipal dispatch tool.
