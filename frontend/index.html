    How it works

    Where the data comes from

    Every request residents made to Ottawa's 311 service since January 2025, published by the City as open data. Only the dates, request type, ward, and how it was submitted are used: no addresses.

    Ward report card

    The map shades each ward by its number of 311 requests in the last 12 months. Use the menu on the map to shade by typical days to close or by unusual jumps instead, and choose a problem in step 2 to see the map for just that problem. Click a ward to see what its residents report most, and how many days those requests typically take to close there compared with the whole city. These are counts from City records, not predictions.

    How long will my request take?

    A machine-learning model trained on over 350,000 closed requests estimates the usual time to close, and a time that 9 in 10 similar requests close within. Tested on requests it had never seen, about 90% closed within the "9 in 10" time. For types marked ⚠️ the answer is read from recent requests instead. If you have already reported it, enter how many days you have waited: the answer then comes from past requests that were still open after that long.

    Unusual this week

    A request type is flagged when a ward gets at least 5 this week, at least 3 times its usual weekly number, and a jump that size would almost never happen by chance. A cluster of leak or street-light reports can be an early sign of one larger problem.

    What this can't tell you

    "Closed" is when the City marked a request closed. In May 2026 the City closed about 130,000 old requests in one bulk clean-up, so for about half the request types the older closing dates are not real. Those types are marked ⚠️, and their times use only requests reported after the clean-up.
